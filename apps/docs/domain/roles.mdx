## Data ownership

- A user signs in via **Clerk** and selects or creates an **organization**. On their first
  action, the org and membership are provisioned automatically (see
  [Multitenancy & Auth](/architecture/multitenancy)).
- **All data is owned by the organization**, not the individual user. Members share the herd.
- A signed-in user with no selected org is blocked with `OrgNotSelectedError` — they must
  pick or create one first.

## Roles

`membership.role` is one of:

| Role | Default? |
| --- | --- |
| `admin` | No |
| `member` | Yes — assigned on JIT provisioning |

## Current authorization gap

<Warning>
  **No authorization gating is implemented yet.** Every authenticated organization member can
  call every server action equally. The `role` field is provisioned and returned by
  `getCurrentOrganization()`, but it is not yet consulted anywhere.
</Warning>

**Planned.** Gate destructive and administrative operations — bulk delete, member management,
organization settings — on `role === "admin"`. This is the natural first consumer of the
role field and will arrive with the **Team** feature.
